Special Beyaynetu

450

A vibrant, traditional combination platter of slow-simmered lentil, chickpea, and seasonal vegetable stews served fresh over authentic sourdough injera.

Key Ingredients

  • Misir Wot (Spiced Red Lentils)
  • Kik Alicha (Yellow Split Peas)
  • Gomen (Steamed Collard Greens)
  • Atkilt Wot (Cabbage, Carrots & Potatoes)
  • Shiro Wot (Seasoned Chickpea Stew)
  • Fresh Sourdough Injera
