    🛒 About Addis Market

    Addis Market is a simple and friendly shopping list application designed for everyday shopping in Ethiopia.

    It helps you organize the items you need, keep track of what you have already bought, and see your total spending in Ethiopian Birr (ETB).

    🇪🇹 Shopping in Ethiopia

    Addis Market uses Ethiopian Birr prices and familiar everyday products such as coffee, bread, vegetables, fruits, water and household items.

    The project demonstrates how web technology can make everyday shopping easier and more organized.

    ✨ What You Can Do

    • Add shopping items
    • Set prices and quantities
    • Automatically categorize items
    • Mark items as bought
    • Search your shopping list
    • Track your total ETB cost
