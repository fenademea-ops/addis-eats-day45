Our Story & Heritage

Bringing authentic Ethiopian traditions, hand-blended spices, and community dining to the heart of Bole, Addis Ababa.

Rooted in Tradition

At Habesha Eatery, food is more than nourishment—it is a celebration of community, history, and warmth. Established with a passion for preserving traditional Ethiopian culinary arts, every dish we prepare reflects recipes passed down through generations.

From slow-simmered Doro Wat spiced with our house-blend berbere to freshly baked, 100% teff Injera, we source our ingredients locally to ensure authentic flavor in every bite.

Authentic Spices

Sourced directly from local farmers and blended in-house for distinct, rich flavor.

Traditional Hospitality

Experience the spirit of Gursha and community warmth in a traditional setting.

Coffee Ceremony

Enjoy our daily fresh coffee roasting ritual served with frankincense and popcorn.
